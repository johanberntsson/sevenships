{at 6 16}{text}The great kingdom of Anatolia, ruled from Hattusa in the highlands,
whose kings called the Pharaoh their brother. They hold the copper of
the mountains and are famous for their chariots, which cost them less.
An inland power, strong in war on land. Led by the computer, they
build the Palace of Knossos before any other wonder.
