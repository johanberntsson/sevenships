{at 6 17}{text}The merchant cities of the Levant coast, Ugarit, Byblos, Sidon and
Tyre, the middlemen between Egypt, Hatti and the Aegean. They own the
cedar and the purple dye, and pay mercenaries rather than raise armies:
gold buys them more. Led by the computer, they build the Great Harbour
of Ugarit before any other wonder.
