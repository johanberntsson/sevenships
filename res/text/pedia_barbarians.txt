{at 6 16}{text}Bands of warriors who belong to no kingdom appear far from anyone and
attack whatever is near, and burn the cities they take. They come more
often, and more of them, on the harder levels.
From 1200 BC the Sea Peoples come by galley, two raiders a ship, to the
richest cities by the sea, more and more often towards the end. They
attack from their ships, sink merchant ships and burn what they take.
A people that loses its capital may break up: its far cities go over to
the rebels, barbarian cities with a guard, which can be taken.
