{at 6 16}{text}The kingdom of the Nile in its New Kingdom, ruled from Waset, which
the Greeks called Thebes. The river's flood makes it the richest land
in grain and men, and its cities grow faster than anyone's. It has no
cedar for ships and no tin for bronze, and must trade for both. Led by
the computer, it builds the Temple of Karnak before any other wonder.
