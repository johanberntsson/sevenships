{at 6 16}{text}The palace kingdoms of mainland Greece, behind their great walls:
Mycenae, Tiryns, Pylos, Thebes. Their hills give wine and olive oil
but little grain, so they look to the sea, trading with the coasts and
islands round them and raiding them as well. Their ships are cheaper
to build. Led by the computer, they build the Lion Gate before any
other wonder.
